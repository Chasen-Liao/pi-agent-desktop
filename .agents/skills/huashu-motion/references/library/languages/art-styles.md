# art-styles — the same idea in the language of a movement

Starry-night swirls, Ukiyo-e waves, Bauhaus geometry, Ghibli skies, ink wash, pop-art halftone,
Rembrandt light — 35 art styles drawn in code by the huashu-art-motion engine; "recursion explained
in genres"; a cube whose faces are styles.

**Says:** *culture, delight, surprise; this is more than a tool.*

**Use when:** showcases, cultural/educational content, the unexpected direction (C) for a brand, a
"same thing in N styles" structure. **Avoid when:** the product must be seen as it is.

## Build

Use the art-motion style cards and engine (`huashu-art-motion`, references/风格配方/INDEX.md). Each
style has its own drawing rules and a material-driven transition; keep the object's identity across
styles so the switch reads as a transformation.

## Seen in Oct 2026

- **Constant subject, changing eras.** One composition (a logo in the centre, a fixed corner HUD)
  re-skinned through art eras at 0.5–1 s each, ending back on the modern end card. Weak when each era
  only changes colour: each era needs its own motion grammar too (huashu-art-motion's grammar cards).
- **Painted scroll.** An AI-painted landscape or ink scroll, slow push and parallax, calligraphy
  appearing with the narration or stroke order. Calm, cultural; a good C direction for
  Chinese-language products.
