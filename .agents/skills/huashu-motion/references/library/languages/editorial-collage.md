# editorial-collage — evidence on paper (Vox / print / engraving)

Real documents, screenshots, photos and clippings, torn paper, highlighter strokes, circled lines,
halftone and engraving textures; or a print-design reimagining (the engraving-style Claude ad).

**Says:** *this is real; look at this line; here's the argument.* Journalistic authority, editorial taste.

**Use when:** commentary, news, case studies, history, "here is what they said", brands with an
editorial voice. **Avoid when:** there is no real material (it turns into fake evidence).

## Signature

1. Real sources as physical objects on a surface: paper texture, drop shadows, slight rotation.
2. The highlighter stroke / circle / underline that draws on as the narrator says it.
3. Push-in to the exact line; everything else dims.
4. Halftone / engraving / riso textures; serif display + a typewriter or grotesk.
5. Hand-painted elements on top of photos (brush strokes, arrows, stickers) — "paint on the image".

## Camera · rhythm · easing

Hand-held-like micro drift; quick slides of new paper in (`power3.out` with 2–4° rotation settle);
fast pace for ads (shots 0.5–1.5 s), slower for explainers.

## Sound

Paper slides, pen squeaks, stamp, camera shutter; the narration on top; a punchy track for ads.

## Build

`vox-collage` template for argument films; highlight rects measured from real frames (`om look`),
never guessed.

## Breaks when

Fake documents; unreadable sources; texture so heavy that the content disappears.
