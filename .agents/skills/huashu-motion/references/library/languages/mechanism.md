# mechanism — one sentence, one transformation (3Blue1Brown)

Mathematical and algorithmic ideas shown by continuous transformation of the same objects: an
equation rearranges, a curve deforms, vectors rotate, tokens flow through attention. Fourier
epicycles drawing a pelican (Google's Gemini 4 Argon demo) live here.

**Says:** *this is why it works; you can see it for yourself.*

**Use when:** AI/ML, algorithms, science, finance mechanics, "how X works under the hood".
**Avoid when:** launches with nothing to explain, stories, fast hype.

## Signature

1. Dark ground (or the brand's), bright strokes; LaTeX-quality maths.
2. Objects transform rather than being replaced (TransformMatchingShapes thinking).
3. Colour carries identity: the same quantity keeps its colour through every step.
4. One new idea per sentence of narration; the picture changes when the sentence does.
5. A final frame that shows the whole mechanism at once.

## Camera · rhythm · easing

Slow, deliberate: `power2.inOut`, 0.8–1.5 s per transformation, holds while the narrator explains.
Camera moves only to follow the object in focus.

## Sound

Narration drives it; music is a soft bed; few effects (soft ticks for counting, a shimmer on the
insight).

## Build

The `math-3b1b` template for formula/curve explainers; Canvas or SVG for custom mechanisms;
`OM.cue('word')` anchors each transformation to its sentence.

## Breaks when

Labels pop in without motion meaning; ten things move at once; the transformation is faster than the
explanation.
